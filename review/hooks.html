Hook Picker

For each song: play the candidates and tick every one that's a real hook (the bits you'd recognise it by) - as many as you like. "None" if none of them are.
Sounds: ▶ S plays the song's melodic layer (no drums/bass) where its high end is most exposed. Drag the low/high cut until only the sound you want is left (the spectrum shows where it lives), then "Save this sound". Picks save as you go and replace the automatic guess.
